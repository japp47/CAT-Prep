# Mock review section layouts (24 Oct 2026)

## What the CAT pattern fixes

CAT 2025 had three sections in a fixed order and 40 minutes per section. Its three slots had 24 VARC, 22 DILR and 22 Quant questions. The CAT 2026 notification keeps the same three-section, 40-minute format; use CAT 2025 as the latest completed-paper baseline until the 2026 paper is available.

The exam authority fixes section names, timing and total questions in the paper. It does not promise a fixed count for each VA subtype, DILR set type or Quant topic. These mixes must stay editable in mock analysis.

## Starting layouts

- **VARC:** 24 questions: 4 RC passages × 4, plus 8 VA questions. Seed with two each of para summary, sentence insertion, odd sentence out and para jumble. That is a familiar layout, but the actual VA mix can differ. Mark question format separately because TITA counts can vary.
- **DILR:** 22 questions across 5 sets, seeded as 5, 5, 4, 4, 4. Set styles are editable (arrangements, games, distribution, scheduling, tables/graphs, caselets, routes/networks, Venn diagrams, mixed/other). Sets may mix DI and LR, so the default style is "Unsorted" rather than a made-up fixed DI/LR split.
- **Quant:** 22 questions. Seed a recent-paper estimate of Arithmetic 9, Algebra 7, Geometry and mensuration 3, Number systems 2 and Modern mathematics 1. Let students edit every count and include Other. Recent CAT 2025 slots differed, so these are a convenience, not a claimed exam rule.

## Product decision

The mock review's test-kind choices include VARC, DILR and Quant sectionals. For a full mock, past paper or official IIM mock, the student selects the section to analyse. Each section shows its own layout editor and uses the selected topic/set labels in the question grid and findings. Counts remain adjustable to match coaching mocks and future CAT papers.
