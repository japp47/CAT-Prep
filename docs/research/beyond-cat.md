# Beyond the exam: thinking practice, community and reminders (23 Oct 2026)

The owner proposed three directions on 23 Oct 2026. This note keeps what was proposed, the product review of it, and what was adopted. Specs are in `docs/feature.md` (F56 to F78), the order in `docs/plan.md` Part 3, risks and decisions in `docs/analyze.md`.

## 1. What the owner proposed

**A. Three experiences on one learning engine.**
- **CAT Coach**: VARC, DILR and Quant practice, the error log, the redo queue, and proof that improvement transfers to unseen questions.
- **Thinking Arcade**: short logic and number puzzles (Sudoku, Queens, Tango, KenKen or Kakuro, chess puzzles, tournament and schedule puzzles), each tied to the skill it trains.
- **Manager Lab**: founder simulator, 60-second brief, mini case interview, manager's daily dilemma.
- DILR ideas: Set Selection Arena (rank four sets before solving, then compare the guess with what happened), DILR Replay (solve on a board, replay the deduction chain), Reality-to-DILR (real tables become caselets), Data Detective (what the data does and does not support).
- Quant ideas: Quant Debugger (find the first wrong step), Same Math Different World, Estimate First, Quant in Real Life, "Why this method?" after every question.
- The thread through all of it: **Reality → Reasoning** (an article or chart → VARC, DILR, Quant, a decision), and one loop: capture the mistake → find the gap → practise a variation → test on something unseen.

**B. Community.** A doubt forum as the default, public communities, invite-only study groups. Ask from a mistake, show your reasoning, compare approaches, accepted answers become a searchable library, link a solved doubt back to practice. Student to student, no coaching answers. Real access control for private groups.

**C. Reminders and accountability.** Daily reminder at a chosen time, streak at risk, recovery after missed days, community activity, weekly review. Personal, never guilt. Count real practice, not app opens. A Go notification service with preferences, scheduling, deduplication and rate limits; in-app inbox first, web push opt-in, email later.

## 2. Product review

### What is strong
- **Practising decisions, not only answers.** Set selection is the most under-trained CAT skill: toppers say the DILR score is decided in the first 5 minutes, by which sets are picked. No free product measures it. It fits the existing mock wizard (F12), which already asks "was this skip doable?".
- **The mistake loop extends naturally to Quant.** The Debugger is F42 (error causes) applied to working steps.
- **Community anchored to our own questions.** A forum is a commodity. Discussion that hangs off a question the student just did, and opens only after they attempt it, is not.
- **Reminders tied to the student's own plan.** 99ile already knows the minutes per weekday (Plan and time) and the next step (F41). A reminder can say what to do and open it.

### What changes, and why
| Proposed | Changed to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Three experiences as equals | **CAT Coach is the product.** The Arcade is a small daily habit inside it. Manager Lab waits until after CAT 2027 | DILR and Quant are not built yet, and students start serious prep in Jan to Mar 2027. Every week spent elsewhere delays the reason anyone comes |
| Manager Lab as a parallel track | **The manager track after the exam, in two seasons**: getting in (WAT, GD, PI, cases; Dec 2027 to Mar 2028) and ready for the MBA (basics, dilemmas, founder simulator; Apr to Jun 2028). Added PI prep from your own story (F76), GD practice (F77) and MBA-ready basics (F78) | That is when a CAT student actually needs it: shortlists arrive in January and WAT, GD and PI run to March. The 60-second brief is WAT and PI practice. Same audience, right moment |
| Ten games | **One daily puzzle, four kinds to start**: tournament table, schedule, Queens, a number grid | Research on game training finds transfer only to closely related skills. Puzzles that are DILR formats (tournaments, scheduling, constraint placement) have the best chance. Generated by code with a solver that proves a unique answer, so they are free and tested. Sudoku and chess are off-skill and widely available; not built |
| Public communities and study groups at launch | **Question discussions and the doubt forum first, then private study groups, public communities last and only if moderation keeps up** | Public groups need moderators we do not have. An empty or spammy forum is worse than none (coaching promoters are the known spam source in CAT spaces). Private groups moderate themselves |
| Image uploads in doubts | **Text and maths (KaTeX) first, images later** | Storage cost, abuse scanning and moderation. Most doubts on our questions need only the question link |
| Web push as a later step | **In-app inbox first, web push for students who installed 99ile, the calendar reminder for everyone else** | iPhone web push needs iOS 16.4+ and 99ile added to the Home Screen; the prompt only appears after a tap. Many iPhone students will never see a push, so it cannot be the only channel |
| A streak that breaks | **Forgiving streak**: one free day a week, a missed day is never red, a restart plan after three missed days | Principle 1 and F51 already say a missed day is simply empty |

### What we add (not in the proposal)
- **Spoiler gate.** A question's discussion opens only after the student attempts it. "Learn together, solve independently" becomes a rule in code, not a slogan.
- **Teach-back counts.** Writing an accepted explanation for someone else's doubt counts as a review of that question in your own redo queue (explaining is one of the strongest ways to learn).
- **Another way.** Every question already has our written solution. The community adds alternative methods under it, voted, so the library grows without more content work.
- **Group streak, not a global leaderboard.** In a study group, the group keeps its streak if most members practised. The global leaderboard (F37) becomes group-only: comparing with friends motivates; comparing with 10,000 strangers mostly discourages.
- **Set difficulty from real times.** Arena sets start with the author's time estimate and are recalibrated from students' actual times once sync data exists (aggregates only, no one's data shown).
- **North-star metric.** "Mistakes re-solved correctly on a later try, per active student per week." It measures the loop, not visits. Guardrails: study reminders turned off by under 20% of those who turned them on; 70% of doubts answered within 24 hours.

### Not doing
- Direct messages between students (harassment risk, no way to moderate privately).
- A general social feed, follower counts or likes on profiles.
- Tutors or coaching institutes answering for pay, or ads in the community.
- Infinite play in the Arcade: one daily puzzle plus a small practice pool, no timers that punish.
- Claiming that puzzles raise CAT scores. We measure it (unseen DILR sets before and after) before saying anything.
- More than one study reminder a day, or any reminder during quiet hours.

## 3. Facts checked (23 Oct 2026)
- **iPhone web push**: iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later, the site added to the Home Screen with a manifest, a service worker, and the permission asked after a user tap; some developers report pushes that stop working. Sources: OneSignal "Web push for iOS", Pushpad "iOS special requirements for web push notifications".
- **Indian IT Rules 2021**: a forum where users talk to each other is a social media intermediary. Below 50 lakh Indian users it is not "significant", but it still needs published rules, a grievance officer, acknowledgement of a complaint within 24 hours and resolution within 15 days. Later amendments exist; check the current text before launch. Sources: PRS India, Stanford WILMap, EFF.
- **DPDP**: under-18s are children; processing their data needs verifiable parental consent (Rule 10, DigiLocker or existing records). Community stays 18 and over, as sync already is. Sources: MediaNama, Mondaq, dpdpa.com Rule 12 page.
- **Competitors**: article-to-RC labs, Quant games and mock-to-practice links already exist elsewhere (AthenaPrep, CATin). The difference has to be the connected loop and set selection, not the existence of a feature.
- **Old CAT forums**: PaGaLGuY began in 2002 as the CAT and IIM forum; discussion has since spread to Telegram groups and Reddit. Lesson taken: a forum lives or dies on answer speed and spam control, not on features.
